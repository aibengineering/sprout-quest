# Characters on the authored routes

These regional roles are implemented for the upcoming 0.3.7 release and reflected in the sibling Story Bible.

| Area | Character / destination | Play and payoff |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet Glade | Veyra’s awakening presence; Oswin welcomes the hero in Sowerby. | Movement and combat introduction; optional statue spur. |
| Sowerby | Clover, Oswin and returning human neighbours. | Cooking, conversations, Bram’s incremental building requests and independent upgrades. |
| East Road / Sunny Meadow | Poppy’s Secret Grove; Moss’s ridge stop. | Rescue and chase earn Trail Boots. Moss returns to bake beside Clover and the garden. |
| Whisper Woods | Bram’s old logging camp. | Noise-sensitive chopping and rescue; sawmill, village construction and timber shortcuts. |
| Echo Cavern | Pip’s quarry tunnel; Poppy’s Pebbler Hollow branch. | Ten-hit discovery reveals mixed ore and a village tunnel. Poppy’s procession grants Echo Anklet. |
| Glimmer Hollow | Hidden masked fox, marked shard trial and shared den. | Follow her and earn Shadow Scarf, then visit her for six practice lessons at her hidden clearing. |
| Ember Peak | Rook’s western shelter after the Emberwyrm. | Bring the injured hunter home, build his lodge, revisit regional pockets for scaled variants and trophies. |

The fox is the proud masked monster described in the Bible, gathering glimmer to light a den shared with other spirits. She notices the hero’s noisy feet, tests whether they can listen, and offers a quiet gift. The friendship hints at Act 2 without giving the hero its first magical bond or explaining reincarnation. She stays hidden and visitable when Rook arrives; he never meets her and she is never a hunt target. There is no public monster welcome or Sowerby dojo plot.

Shadow Scarf improves dodge speed and distance with an afterimage and adds a short map dash; Echo Anklet still provides two charges. The field gift requires no construction payment. Afterwards Bram discreetly supplies soft targets at her clearing. Three purchased training tiers, six challenge IDs and once-only XP rewards are retained from the former dojo, including old saves; the fox teaches, Bram constructs.

Rook is an affable outsider with buyers back home, injured in an overconfident pursuit of the Emberwyrm. Clover, Poppy and Pip’s misgivings remain quiet conversations. His optional collection system does not gate Moss, the fox or the main ending. Existing residents and hunt progress survive the new post-dragon recruitment gate.

The Bible’s imp/banner quest and Ember Cloak remain future ideas. Poppy remains a recurring emotional thread, rather than being confined to one regional quest.
